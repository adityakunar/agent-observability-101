# Store Hours

| Day       | Opens | Closes |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Monday    | 11:00 | 22:00  |
| Tuesday   | 11:00 | 22:00  |
| Wednesday | 11:00 | 22:00  |
| Thursday  | 11:00 | 22:30  |
| Friday    | 11:00 | 23:30  |
| Saturday  | 10:00 | 23:30  |
| Sunday    | 11:00 | 22:00  |

## Kitchen Last Orders

The kitchen accepts last orders 30 minutes before closing time.
Orders placed after the last-orders cutoff cannot be guaranteed.

## Public Holidays

The store operates reduced hours on public holidays: 12:00–21:00.
Delivery is not available on Christmas Day or New Year's Day.
Collection only on those days, subject to staff availability.

## Peak Hours

Expect longer wait times during:

- Friday and Saturday evenings: 18:00–21:00 (add 10–15 minutes to all estimates).
- Sunday lunch: 12:00–14:00 (add 5–10 minutes to estimates).

## Ordering Channels

| Channel        | Availability                                      |
|----------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Phone          | During all opening hours                          |
| Online / Web   | During all opening hours, up to 30 min before close |
| App            | 24/7 for pre-scheduled orders up to 24 hours ahead |
