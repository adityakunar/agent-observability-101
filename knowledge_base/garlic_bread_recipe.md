# Garlic Bread Recipe

## Classic Lantern Pizza-Style Garlic Bread

### Ingredients

- 1 baguette or Italian bread loaf
- 100g unsalted butter, softened
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt to taste
- Optional: 50g mozzarella cheese, shredded

### Steps

1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F).
2. Slice the baguette in half lengthways.
3. Mix softened butter with minced garlic, parsley, oregano, and a pinch of salt.
4. Spread the garlic butter evenly over both cut halves.
5. If adding cheese, scatter mozzarella over the top.
6. Wrap loosely in foil and bake for 10 minutes.
7. Open the foil for the final 3–5 minutes to crisp the edges.
8. Slice into portions and serve immediately.

### Notes

- For extra flavour, add a pinch of chilli flakes to the butter mixture.
- Garlic bread can be prepared up to 4 hours ahead and refrigerated before baking.
- Preparation time: 10 minutes. Baking time: 13–15 minutes. Serves 4.
